Advanced Subtitle Translator & Styler

Drag & drop or Click to upload .srt / .ass file

How to Use — Translator

  1. Select your target language and customize the font, size & style.
  2. Upload either a .srt or .ass subtitle file.
  3. Click “Translate & Apply Styles” and wait until it reaches 100%.
  4. Click the appearing Download button to save your translated subtitle!

Multi-Subtitle Converter

Drag & drop or Click to select multiple files

How to Use — Converter

  1. Tap the upload box and select multiple subtitle files (10 or more .srt / .ass files at once).
  2. Choose your target format from the dropdown menu.
  3. Click the “Convert Files” button.
  4. Once converted, click “Download Converted ZIP” to save all files as a single ZIP!
